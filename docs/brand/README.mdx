## Logo

The mark is a ledger page that becomes an R. There is one mark, drawn from one set of vectors at
every size, and the name is outlined, so no logo needs a font installed. The name is always
lowercase, even at the start of a sentence.

<LogoCards />

### Clear space and small sizes

Keep one ledger row of clear space around the mark, marked **x** below. Its lines scale with it, so
it stays as light at 16px as it is at 48px.

<LogoConstruction />

## App icon

The icon is the mark on a parchment or forest plate. Parchment is the default, on the desktop app and
in browser tabs. Inside the app there is no plate: the mark takes the theme's text color.

<AppIcons />

## Color

Five colors, in about these proportions: mostly Parchment and Forest, with Pine and Sage in support
and Brass used sparingly. They are for the icon, the logo and marketing. The app itself is neutral
([look rules](../rules/look.md)). Click a color to copy its hex.

### Data colors

The app's charts draw from eight hues of their own, led by blue and checked for color-blind
separation on each theme's surface. Use them for charts only.

## Typography

Geist sets words and figures, and Geist Mono sets code. The logo is the
exception: its name is Nunito Bold, for the rounded ends, and its tagline Inter Medium, both outlined,
so they are drawings and not type to set. Don't use either anywhere else.

## Changing the brand

`pnpm brand:generate` draws every logo and icon from `scripts/generate-brand.ts`, and writes the
color tokens and the kit from `docs/brand/tokens/colors.json`. Change those, not the generated files.
The app's themes, Day and Night, are the tokens in `packages/ui/src/styles.css`, and they use none of these colors.
